# Data and source rights

The MIT license covers this repository’s software, original documentation, and generated synthetic fixtures. It grants no rights to third-party historical scans, reference transcriptions, provider outputs, or other external material used with the harness. Those materials are not distributed in this repository.

The example year is invented. Its image files are generated color blocks used to exercise image transport and hashing, not historical scans or a visual-recognition benchmark. Scripted responses test workflow behavior, not OCR accuracy.

Real-source acceptance results are described in aggregate. The private acceptance corpus and edition are not included. Users must establish their own rights to process and redistribute external inputs and outputs.
